Zero Personal Data Collection & 100% Offline Processing

Image Crop does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, identifiers, or photo contents. All cropping, transformation, filtering, and export functions operate completely locally on your device without communicating with any external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Image Crop ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") manages your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying fully with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information Collection and Use

We believe in complete data minimization:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identifiers.
  • No Account or Registration: You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the App.
  • No Device or Location Tracking: We do not collect GPS location data, hardware serial numbers, advertising IDs (AAID), or IP addresses.
  • No Analytics or Crash Tracking: The App does not integrate third-party telemetry, tracking libraries, or analytics SDKs.

3. Handling of Photos, Images & Media

The core functionality of the App is to crop, scale, rotate, filter, and save images. Here is how your media is handled:

  • Zero-Permission Photo Selection: The App utilizes the modern, secure Android Photo Picker system (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). This guarantees that the App only accesses the individual photo you explicitly select, with zero access to your broader photo library or filesystem.
  • On-Device Local Processing: All image manipulations—including zooming, rotation, rule-of-thirds alignment, color matrix filtering (Grayscale, Sepia, High Contrast, Warm, Cool), and bitmap rendering—take place entirely in volatile device memory (RAM) using your local CPU/GPU.
  • No Server Uploads: Your images are never transmitted over the Internet, uploaded to cloud storage, or seen by any third party.
  • Saving to Device: When you tap "Save", the cropped image is written directly to your device's standard public directory (Pictures/ImageCropApp) via the Android MediaStore API.

4. Device Permissions

The App strictly adheres to the principle of least privilege:

Permission / API Type Purpose
Android Photo Picker System Picker Allows you to choose an image to edit without granting broad storage read permissions.
MediaStore API Scoped Storage Allows the App to write the cropped image into the device's public Pictures collection.
Internet Access None The App does not require or request network permissions for core editing functions.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not include:

  • Advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads)
  • Behavioral tracking or analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, Flurry)
  • Social media trackers or marketing plugins
  • Cloud processing or external AI/ML APIs for user images

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is intended for a general audience and is fully safe for all age groups. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

7. Data Security and Retention

Because we do not collect or store any user data on servers:

  • There are no user databases or remote accounts to be compromised.
  • All temporary bitmaps in memory are released when the App is closed or the image is discarded.
  • Saved images remain under your full control on your device storage, and can be viewed, shared, or deleted at any time through your preferred gallery application or file manager.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Know / Access: No personal data is stored by us to access.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete any saved photos directly from your device at any time. Uninstalling the application removes all application-specific cached files.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal data or media.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
